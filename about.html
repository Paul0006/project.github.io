WHO AM I?

A little about me

Hi, I'm Pratyush. I'm from India, and I'm currently studying ICT at HZ University of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands. I've always been interested in technology, especially how computers, apps and other devices actually work. I like trying out new things and understanding what happens behind the screen instead of just using them.

Since starting my ICT studies, I've been learning more about programming and web development. I'm still a beginner in many areas, but I enjoy the feeling of finally getting something to work after trying different solutions. Sometimes I get stuck or make mistakes, but that's also how I learn. Building this website has been one way for me to practise HTML and CSS and see what I can create on my own.

Outside of studying, I enjoy gaming, keeping up with new gadgets and learning about space and science. Gaming is something I do to relax, but it also makes me curious about the technology used to create games. Space interests me because there is still so much we don't know, and I think it's exciting how software helps people explore it.

Right now, I don't have my entire future planned out. I want to get better at programming, try different areas of ICT and find out what I enjoy most. My aim is to keep improving, become more confident solving problems and eventually work on technology that I find genuinely interesting.

  • Technology and new gadgets
  • Space and science
  • Gaming
