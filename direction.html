Direction

I do not want to decide too early that there is only one ICT job for me. Right now I am interested in software development, AI, cybersecurity and technology connected to space.

My plan is to use the first part of the programme to build strong programming skills and try different areas. Later I can choose a direction based on what I actually enjoy working on, not only what sounds interesting.

In the future I would like to work on technology that solves difficult problems and keeps giving me new things to learn.
