ICT field of work

One area of ICT that interests me is artificial intelligence and software development. Software is used in everyday apps, businesses, healthcare, science and space systems.

What people do in this field

Software developers create, test and improve programs. They often work in teams with other developers, designers and people who use the software. People working with AI and data may collect data, build models and check whether their results are useful and reliable.

Why it interests me

What attracts me to this field is that it keeps changing. Developers need to learn new skills, communicate clearly and solve problems. I like that what I learn now might later be useful in many industries.

In the future I want to learn more about how ICT companies build software, test their products and use AI to solve real-world problems.
