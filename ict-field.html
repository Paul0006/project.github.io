ICT field of work

One area of ICT that interests me is artificial intelligence and software development. Software is used almost everywhere, from normal apps to scientific and space systems.

What attracts me to this field is that it changes quickly. Developers have to keep learning, solve problems and work with other people. I like the idea that the skills I learn now can later be used in many different industries.

I would like to learn more about how ICT companies develop software and use artificial intelligence to solve real-world problems. I am especially interested in understanding how developers work together on projects and how programming skills are used in different industries.
