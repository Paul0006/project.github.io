Goals & SWOT analysis

My main goal is to build a strong base in programming and find out which ICT direction fits me best. I am especially interested in software development and AI.

My professional goals

  • This year: Understand the basics of HTML, CSS and JavaScript, complete my assignments and work towards earning all 60 first-year credits.
  • During the programme: Work on more projects, improve my problem-solving and teamwork skills, and explore Software Engineering and Data Science.
  • In the future: Use my ICT skills to help build useful software, possibly in AI or space-related technology.

My SWOT analysis

Strengths

I am curious about technology, willing to experiment and motivated to learn. These strengths help me continue when my code does not work the first time.

Weaknesses

I sometimes jump to a new topic before finishing the previous one. To improve, I want to complete one exercise and review my mistakes before moving on.

Opportunities

HZ offers projects, feedback and ICT directions to explore. I can use these opportunities to practise my skills and discover what suits me.

Threats

Poor planning and leaving assignments until the last moment could affect my results. I want to start tasks earlier and set small weekly goals.

How I plan to succeed at HZ

  1. Practise programming several times each week and finish small challenges before starting new ones.
  2. Plan assignment work before the deadline and divide larger tasks into smaller steps.
  3. Ask questions and use teacher feedback when I get stuck.
  4. Keep track of my exams and credits in my dashboard. The first year has 60 EC, and the Study Advice boundary is 45 EC.
  5. Review what went wrong after a mistake so I can avoid repeating it.

By using my curiosity as a strength and working on my planning as a weakness, I can make better progress towards my study and career goals.
