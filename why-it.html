WHY IT?

Why ICT suits me

I chose ICT because I have always been curious about technology and I like discovering how things work. I do not only want to use technology; I want to understand what happens behind it and learn how to create things myself.

I enjoy experimenting even when something does not work the first time. Solving the problem is part of what makes it interesting for me. I am still exploring different directions, especially software development, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity.

What I want to learn

  • Write cleaner and better code
  • Build useful software
  • Understand AI and data
  • Learn more about cybersecurity

My long-term direction

I would like to use ICT in a field that keeps challenging me. Space technology is one area that interests me because it combines software, data and science.
